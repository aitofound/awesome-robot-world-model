# 10. Embodiment and Task Perspective

[Home](../../README.md) · [Reading paths](../reading-paths.md) · [Full catalog](../catalog.md)

Manipulation stresses contact, navigation persistent belief, humanoids balance and morphology, driving traffic and rare events, and cross-robot control action semantics.

## Reading questions

- What are the controller and action units?
- What task history must persist?
- Which physical feedback and assistance costs are measured?

## Interpretation boundary

A command-conditioned locomotion policy is not autonomous navigation. Open-loop physical execution is not online correction.
