# 01. Introduction

[Home](../../README.md) · [Reading paths](../reading-paths.md) · [Full catalog](../catalog.md)

The Prediction-to-Control Gap motivates a loop-centered survey. Predictive plausibility matters only through the decision or learning use it enables.

## Reading questions

- What quantity is modeled?
- Who consumes the output?
- Which decision or learner changes?

## Interpretation boundary

Do not infer robot utility from visual realism alone.
