# 13. Frontier Evidence Atlas: 2024–2026

[Home](../../README.md) · [Reading paths](../reading-paths.md) · [Full catalog](../catalog.md)

The frontier atlas locates emerging systems by mechanism, role and bounded evidence. It complements the historical bibliography without creating a pooled leaderboard.

## Reading questions

- Which paper version supports the claim?
- What hardware and task scope was tested?
- Is the artifact availability separate from publication status?

## Interpretation boundary

Source evidence labels are curation judgments, not independently replicated rankings. Open-loop, frozen-model and policy-context cases stay explicit.
