# 02. Positioning Among Existing Surveys

[Home](../../README.md) · [Reading paths](../reading-paths.md) · [Full catalog](../catalog.md)

The survey relates architecture, application and world-model literature to a role-first account of robot learning.

## Reading questions

- What does a related survey organize?
- Which functional distinction does this survey add?

## Interpretation boundary

A role-based organization is not a claim that earlier surveys lacked functional categories.

## Selected annotations

This section states the survey’s argument; follow the neighboring method guides for concrete systems.
